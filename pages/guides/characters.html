Characters & Bosses

Meet Charlie Disco and the five Idol betrayers.

The Cast

Dead As Disco centers on Charlie Disco and the five former bandmates who betrayed him. Each has their own arena, attack style, and personal story arc. Tackle them in any order after the first boss.

Charlie Disco — The Protagonist

Drummer of Dead As Disco. Killed ten years ago, resurrected for one night. Fights to uncover the truth and get revenge. His combat style is a mix of power drumming and rhythm-based martial arts — every punch lands on the beat. Charlie is the most balanced character, effective in all situations.

Arora — The AI Idol

Arena: Harmony Corp Digital Stage

Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆ (Easy)

Music: "Synthetic Dreams" — synth-pop with digital distortion, BPM 135

Arora is a next-gen AI pop idol built by Harmony Corp. Her attack pattern centers on melody projectiles — musical notes that home toward Charlie.

Phase 1: Fires rhythmic note patterns. Watch the beat — dodge on-beat, counterattack during the gap.

Phase 2 (below 50% HP): "Harmony Trap" — area denial that freezes Charlie if he does not Beat Sync. Position near arena edges to avoid it. She is vulnerable for 3 seconds after the trap ends.

Weakness: Arora has no close-range options. Close the distance with a charged heavy attack to break her rhythm and deal free damage.

The Four Former Bandmates

The remaining bosses are Charlie's former bandmates, each now an established music idol. Their attack patterns are tied to their musical styles — a guitarist with sound waves, a bassist with low-frequency attacks, a vocalist with sonic screams, and a keyboardist who creates sonic traps. Each boss has three phases: normal patterns, increased attack speed at 50% HP, and a Beat Sync phase at 25% HP where every attack must be on-beat.

Boss General Tips

  • Always save a Beat Sync for each boss's Beat Sync phase
  • Watch for the pink glow — it means a Beat Sync attack is coming
  • Their stagger window is 2-3 seconds after a full combo
  • Grab and throw them into each other to open safe attack windows
