About Dead As Disco Guide

The best fan resource for Dead As Disco players — covering every boss, every combo, and every secret in the game.

What Is This Site?

DeadAsDiscoGuide.com is an independent fan website created to be the most comprehensive, accurate resource for players of Dead As Disco — the rhythm-combat Beat 'Em Up where every punch syncs to the music.

From day one, our goal has been to build the guide hub that the game deserves: detailed boss strategies, full combo move lists, complete story walkthroughs, and coverage of the 30+ track soundtrack. Whether you're a newcomer to rhythm games or a seasoned Beat 'Em Up player, there's something here for you.

The Game

Dead As Disco puts you in the shoes of Charlie Disco, the late drummer of legendary band Dead As Disco. Ten years after his murder, his former bandmates are reuniting for a concert — and Charlie has risen from the grave to confront them. It's a non-linear story of reunion, revenge, and redemption, choreographed to an original 30+ song soundtrack.

Our Mission

We create detailed, accurate, and genuinely useful guides — written by players who have spent real time with the game. Every boss strategy, every combo chain, every hidden secret has been verified before we publish it.
