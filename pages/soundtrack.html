Soundtrack Guide

Dead As Disco features 30+ original tracks, killer covers, and licensed bangers — all synced to combat. Full track breakdown, unlock conditions, and ratings.

Original Tracks

Main Theme

Dead As Disco Theme

An 80s-inflected rock anthem with a driving beat. The main menu track. BPM: 128.

Boss Theme

Synthetic Dreams

Arora's boss theme. Synth-pop with digital distortion. Mastering this beat makes the Arora fight significantly easier. BPM: 135.

Chapter 3

Last Dance

A mournful track for flashback sequences. The beat slows during emotional moments — pay attention to these cues. BPM: 95.

Final Boss

The Encore (Reunion)

The climactic track with three sections matching the three boss phases. BPM: 140-160.

Infinite Disco

Endless Night

Default Infinite Disco track. High-energy dance-pop that scales difficulty as the session progresses. BPM: 138.

My Music

Bring Your Own Beats

Import any track. The game analyzes the beat and generates a custom combat chart. Works with any genre.

My Music Feature

The My Music feature analyzes any track in your library and generates a custom combat chart synced to its beat. The game detects beat position, BPM changes, track sections (intro/verse/chorus/bridge), dynamics, and energy level for difficulty scaling.

This turns any song into a custom playable level. Create challenges for friends or practice specific tracks for Infinite Disco high scores.
