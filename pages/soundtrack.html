My Music Feature

Dead As Disco supports custom soundtrack import. Use the My Music feature to replace any in-game track with your own songs.

  • Supported formats: MP3, WAV, OGG
  • Replace any track in Story or Infinite Disco mode
  • Import via the Options menu in the Soundtrack tab
  • Full Beat Sync support with custom songs
