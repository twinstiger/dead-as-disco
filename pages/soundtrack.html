My Music Feature

The My Music feature analyzes any track in your library and generates a custom combat chart synced to its beat. The game detects beat position, BPM changes, track sections (intro/verse/chorus/bridge), dynamics, and energy level for difficulty scaling.

This turns any song into a custom playable level. Create challenges for friends or practice specific tracks for Infinite Disco high scores.
