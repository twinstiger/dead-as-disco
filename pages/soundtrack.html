My Music Feature

Dead As Disco supports custom soundtrack import. Use the My Music feature to replace any in-game track with your own songs — perfect for adding your personal favorite tracks to the fight.

  • Supported formats: MP3, WAV, OGG
  • Replace any track in Story or Infinite Disco mode
  • Import via the Options menu in the Soundtrack tab
  • Full Beat Sync support with custom songs

Track Ratings

Rating System: Tracks are rated on BPM alignment with combat, musical memorability, and how well they capture each character's personality. Boss themes all feature a unique Beat Sync Phase — a moment in the fight where the music shifts and the player must react to new rhythmic cues.

Hardest Track: "Five Stars" (all five Idols) — the BPM shifts between each Idol's theme, requiring players to constantly readjust their timing mid-combo.
